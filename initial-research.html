User interview questions

8 questions for students
  1. Can you walk me through your placement journey so far?
  2. What platforms, apps or channels do you use during placement?
  3. Tell me about the last company you applied to. What steps did you go through?
  4. What is the most difficult or frustrating part of placement? Why?
  5. How do you keep track of applications, deadlines, interviews and documents?
  6. Have you ever missed something important during placement? What happened?
  7. Does anything about the placement platform cause you stress?
  8. Did you use the placement platform on mobile?
5 questions for the placement cell
  1. Walk me through a drive, from the company’s first contact to offers.
  2. How do you communicate updates to students?
  3. Why WhatsApp over other platforms?
  4. Which information do you send repeatedly?
  5. Which tasks would you automate with the right tool?

Application detailsResumesLock-screen widgetGmail sync: POD, LinkedIn, NaukriAI chatbot
ExploredPlugin that turns a Figma resume into an ATS-checked resumeView Resume Radar on Figma ↗
Why the mobile appThe problem shows up after applying, and updates reach students on their phones through WhatsApp and Gmail. A desktop plugin would miss them. Students apply in too many places for a plugin to cover, so its best idea, scanning the job page, became a capture widget on the phone.

Whiteboarding the first flow

Download→Gmail sign-in→Connect Gmail→Student Passport→Install plugin→Home

Why not Notion or Sheets?

The status updates itself. Notes stay yours.

App or just notifications?

Most: glance at notifications. A few: open the app for resumes and AI help.
